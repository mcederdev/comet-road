COMET ROAD

Fly a ship down a road of floating tiles through deep space. Steer, jump the gaps, ride the loops and switch between blue and orange to land on the right tiles. 500 tracks, six tiers, one mistake and you're stardust.

Coming soon to theApp Store Free to play on iPhone and iPad

WHAT'S ON THE ROAD

500 TRACKS

Six tiers, from Beginner to Impossible. Every track is the same for every player and every one has been checked to be beatable, so times are comparable.

DAILY TRACK

A new track every day at midnight, the same for everyone. Its tier follows the week: Beginner on Monday, up to Impossible on Saturday, and a surprise on Sunday.

UNIQUE TRACKS

Pick a tier and fly a track nobody has flown before. Each one has its own six-character code, and there are billions of them.

LEADERBOARDS AND ICLOUD

Race for the best times on Game Center leaderboards. Your progress syncs between your devices through iCloud. No account, no sign-in, and no internet needed to play.

SWITCH PHASE TO SURVIVE

Your ship is either blue or orange, and it only lands on tiles of its own color. Grey tiles take anyone.

Flip phase at the right moment, mid-jump if you have to, and the road opens up. Flip too late and you fall straight through.

SIX TIERS, NEW TRICKS IN EACH

Clear three out of four tracks in a tier and the next one opens. Each new tier brings something you haven't seen yet.

  1. BEGINNER60 tracks

    Gentle tracks to learn the ship: gaps, tall blocks, boost tiles, slow tiles and hot red tiles.

  2. EASY90 tracks

    Striped roads with blue and orange lanes side by side, and roads that curve away. Stay in your lane, or switch as you cross.

  3. MEDIUM100 tracks

    Flyovers, and curves that change color as they go. Look ahead and pick the road that goes on.

  4. HARD100 tracks

    Loops, slow pads to hop between, and bridges where you change color in mid-air. Going straight and jumping won't get you through.

  5. EXTREME90 tracks

    Pistons that rise and fall, even along winding roads. Arrive when they are down, or jump on top, with fewer places to rest.

  6. IMPOSSIBLE60 tracks

    Platforms that slide from side to side over the gaps, with everything back to back and no rest. The last tracks take an evening, but every one can be beaten.

REASONS TO COME BACK

MEDALS AND GHOSTS

Every track has bronze, silver and gold times. Your best run is saved as a ghost ship, so you can race yourself and see exactly where you lost time.

DAILY STREAK

Fly the Daily Track day after day to build a streak. Every seventh day in a row gives you bonus continues. An optional reminder can nudge you once a day.

THE HANGAR

Fly under six different skies at launch, from an asteroid belt to a frozen moon, with more arriving in updates, and earn seven ship shapes, from a flying saucer to an old-style rocket, plus glowing trails by clearing tracks, winning gold medals and keeping your streak. More ships arrive in updates too. Each theme has its own music too. Themes change the look and the sound, never the track.

SHARE A TRACK

Send the code of a unique track to a friend, or enter theirs, and see who flies it faster.

ORIGINAL CHIPTUNE SOUNDTRACK

Every space theme has its own retro 8-bit track, from icy bells on the frozen moon to fast and happy beats in the comet's tail. The music grows brighter and louder as your ship picks up speed.

TOUCH OR CONTROLLER

Play with on-screen controls, or connect a game controller or keyboard.

FREE, WITH FAIR ADS

Watch an ad only when you want something, like a second chance after a crash. No banners, and never an ad during play. Remove Ads is a single purchase. Optional extras: the Supporter Pack unlocks every ship and trail, and special space themes can be bought one at a time or together in the Space Pack, which includes new ones as they arrive.
