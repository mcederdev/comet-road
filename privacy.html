Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version: You can play Comet Road without an account, and then your progress stays on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Apple, your progress syncs through your own iCloud, and your best times appear on public leaderboards under a callsign you pick. The game never asks for your name or email. The game shows optional ads from Google AdMob when you choose to watch one, and Google may collect some data to show and measure those ads. You can say no to tracking and still play everything.

1. Who is responsible

Comet Road is made and published by an independent developer, Magnus Cederberg, based in Sweden ("I", "me"). I am the controller for any personal data handled in connection with the app, as described below. You can reach me at mcederberg90@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Comet Road app for iPhone and iPad and this website.

2. Data that stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • your best time on each level,
  • which levels you have finished, and
  • whether sound is turned on or off.

If you don't sign in, this data is never sent to me or to anyone else. If you have iCloud or device backups turned on, Apple may include it in your backup under Apple's own terms. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Optional account, iCloud sync and leaderboards

Signing in is optional. Everything in the game works without it. If you tap Sign in and use Sign in with Apple, the following applies.

What Sign in with Apple shares

Comet Road does not ask Apple for your name or your email address, so it never receives them. Apple gives the app only an anonymous user identifier that is unique to Comet Road. The app keeps this identifier in your device's Keychain, and uses it to recognise your save and to check that you are still signed in.

Your progress in iCloud

When you are signed in, your cleared tracks and best times are saved in your own private iCloud storage through Apple's CloudKit, so they follow you to your other devices. This data sits in your iCloud account, not on a server of mine, and I cannot see it. It is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Public leaderboards

When you are signed in, your best times on tracks 1–100 and on Today's track are posted to public leaderboards that every player can see. A leaderboard entry contains:

  • the callsign you choose (your leaderboard name),
  • the track and your time, and
  • an anonymous CloudKit identifier for your iCloud account, so the game knows which entries are yours.

Your callsign is public, so don't use your real name or anything else that identifies you unless you want to. As the developer I can see the leaderboard entries, and I may remove entries or callsigns that are offensive or clearly cheated.

Signing out and deleting your account

  • Sign out stops syncing on that device. Your iCloud save and leaderboard entries stay, so you can sign in again later.
  • Delete account, in the game's account screen, permanently deletes your iCloud save and all of your leaderboard entries. Progress saved on the device itself stays until you delete the app.
  • To also stop Comet Road from using Sign in with Apple, open Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple on your iPhone, choose Comet Road and stop using it.

Other data I collect

None. Comet Road has no analytics of its own and no servers of its own. I don't receive your name, email, location, contacts or anything else from the app. If you email me, I will receive your email address and whatever you write, and I use that only to answer you.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you crash, the game may offer to let you continue from where you were if you watch a short ad. Watching is always your choice. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited.

To show ads, limit how often you see the same one, prevent fraud and measure how ads perform, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and process:

DataExamples
Device identifiersThe Advertising Identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking. Otherwise an app-specific identifier (IDFV).
Approximate locationDerived from your IP address, for example the country or city. Not your precise GPS location.
Device and app informationDevice model, operating system version, language, app version.
Ad interactionsWhich ads were shown, whether you watched or tapped them.
DiagnosticsCrash and performance data from the ads SDK.

Google processes this data under its own policies. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Tracking and personalised ads

The first time it's relevant, iOS asks whether Comet Road may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency). If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the app cannot access your Advertising Identifier and you will see non-personalised ads, or ads based only on the context. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app also shows a consent form from Google before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice later from the game's settings.

You can also reset your Advertising Identifier, or turn off personalised ads from Apple, in your device settings.

5. Legal basis (EU and UK users)

  • Personalised ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and measurement: my legitimate interest in funding a free game, balanced against your privacy.
  • Sign-in, iCloud sync and leaderboards: providing the features you chose to use (performance of a contract). You can stop at any time by signing out or deleting your account.
  • Answering your emails: my legitimate interest in helping you.

6. Children

Comet Road is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads in the app are configured to avoid content that is not suitable for a general audience. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, contact me and I will delete it.

7. How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete the app. Your iCloud save and leaderboard entries are kept until you delete your account in the game. Emails to me are kept for as long as needed to help you and then deleted, at the latest after 24 months. Google's retention periods are described in Google's privacy policy.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You can delete your iCloud save and leaderboard entries yourself with Delete account in the game, and change your callsign in the same screen. The quickest way to control ad-related data is through your device's tracking settings and Google's tools. For anything else, email me and I will reply within 30 days.

If you are in the EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Sweden this is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

9. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, I will update it here and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, for example if the app starts handling new kinds of data, I will also mention it in the app's release notes.
