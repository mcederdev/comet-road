Support

Stuck on a track, found a bug, or have an idea? I'd love to hear from you.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Use the stick on the left to steer. Push it up to go faster and pull it down to brake. Tap JUMP to jump over gaps and onto blocks, and tap PHASE to switch your ship between blue and orange.

What do the different tiles do?

Grey tiles are normal road. Blue tiles only hold a blue ship and orange tiles only an orange one. Yellow tiles give you a speed boost, purple tiles slow you down, and red tiles are hot and destroy your ship. Tall blocks can be landed on, but flying into their side ends your run.

What are the stripes, ramps, loops, pistons and moving platforms?

Each tier after Beginner adds something new. Easy has striped roads with blue and orange lanes side by side. Medium has flyovers, where ramps lift one road over another. Hard has loops, and you need enough speed to stay on at the top. Extreme has pistons that rise and fall, so arrive when they are down or jump on top. Impossible has platforms that slide from side to side over the gaps. The first track in a tier shows a short tip about what's new.

Can I use a game controller or keyboard?

Yes. Connect any controller that works with iPhone or iPad. The left stick steers, the right trigger speeds up, the left trigger brakes, A jumps, and B or the right shoulder button switches phase. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or WASD, space to jump, and X or Shift to switch phase.

How do I unlock more tracks?

There are 500 tracks in six tiers: Beginner, Easy, Medium, Hard, Extreme and Impossible. Every track in an open tier can be played in any order. When you have cleared three out of four tracks in a tier, the next tier opens. The daily track and unique tracks are always open.

When does the daily track change, and how hard is it?

A new daily track arrives at midnight, using the date on your phone, and everyone gets the same one. Its tier follows the week: Beginner on Monday, Easy on Tuesday, Medium on Wednesday, Hard on Thursday, Extreme on Friday and Impossible on Saturday. Sunday is a surprise.

What is a unique track?

A brand new track made just for you. Pick which tier it should be made for (any tier you have opened), and the game builds a track nobody has flown before, with that tier's obstacles. The code in its name tells you which track it is.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. If your device is signed in to iCloud, your cleared tracks and best times sync between your devices on their own, and if you use Game Center, your best times go on the leaderboards.

My progress didn't sync to my other device

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, and that iCloud is turned on for Comet Road in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Open the game on both devices while they are online. Syncing keeps every cleared track and the best time for each track, so nothing is lost.

How do I see the leaderboards, or hide my times?

The leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in Settings → Game Center to post your times. To change your nickname or who can see you, or to turn it off, go to the same place.

How do I delete my saved progress?

Deleting the app removes the copy on your device. To delete the iCloud copy, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage, choose Comet Road and delete its data.

Why am I seeing ads?

Comet Road is free, and ads help pay for it. Some ads you choose to watch, for example to continue after a crash or to skip a track you are stuck on. Now and then a short ad also plays between tracks, but never during play and not in your first ten tracks. There are no banner ads. If you'd rather have no ads at all, you can buy Remove Ads once from the game's menu.

An ad didn't load or I didn't get my reward

If no ad can load, for example because you are offline, the game gives you the reward anyway. If you close a reward ad before it ends, you don't get the reward and can try again. If something still seems wrong, email me and let me know.

I bought Remove Ads, but I see ads on my new phone

Open the game's menu, tap Remove ads and then Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, since Apple handles all payments.

I lost my progress

If iCloud is on, your progress comes back on its own when you install the game on a device signed in to the same Apple Account. If iCloud was off, your progress was saved only on your device. Then it can't be recovered if you deleted the app or moved to a new phone, but restoring an iCloud or computer backup of your device usually brings it back.

Does the game collect my data?

I don't collect any personal data. Your iCloud copy and Game Center scores are handled by Apple, and I can't see your iCloud data. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect some data to show and measure ads. You can say no to tracking when iOS asks. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap Sound on in the menu to switch it to Sound off. The game also respects your phone's volume.
